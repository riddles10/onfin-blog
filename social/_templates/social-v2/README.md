# OnFin social carousel v2 (PostHuman style)

Agreed on 30 Oct 2026:
- Full-bleed photo on every slide.
- Cover: one short hook headline (sentence case, one lime accent phrase) plus an optional small tag pill.
- Other slides: at most one short line (about 8 words). The caption carries the detail, numbers and sources.
- Branding: small bolt avatar and @onfinkm in the top-left corner only. No header bar, no slide counter, no boxes.
- Last slide: a question to drive comments, a follow line and a small risk line.
- Photo sources, in order of preference: public domain and Creative Commons (credit on slide), frames from source videos (credit the channel), licensed stock (Freepik/Magnific), AI images for abstract concepts only (never real people or events).

Component: social.js (SocialSlide). Sample build: social2_sample.py, output in sample/.
