Style 01

Scalping

Seconds to minutes per trade
Collects 2 to 5 pips at a time, dozens of times a session. Volume does the work.
Sticks to the most liquid pairs: EUR/USD, GBP/USD, USD/JPY.
Needs fast execution, raw spreads and a low-latency connection.
Screen time
4 to 8+ hrs/day
Typical stop
3 to 10 pips

Style 02

Swing trading

Days to a few weeks per trade
Rides a move that unfolds over several sessions, using the 1H, 4H and daily charts.
Wider stops, but targets are often 2 to 3 times the stop distance.
Fits a 9-to-5. Watch for overnight swaps and weekend gaps.
Screen time
0.5 to 2 hrs/day
Typical stop
20 to 80 pips

Style 03

Position trading

Weeks to months per trade
Driven by macro themes: interest rate cycles, carry and long-term trends.
Most analysis happens at the weekend. Daily checks take minutes.
Needs real patience: trades often spend more time underwater than in profit.
Screen time
10 to 20 min/day
Typical stop
100+ pips

Avoid the scalp trap

A full-time job, 30 spare minutes and one losing trade is not scalping. It's gambling.

Match the style to the hours you actually have:

Full sessions free, calm under pressureScalping
Day job, can check charts in the eveningSwing
Little time, lots of patiencePosition
