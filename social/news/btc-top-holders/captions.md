# The 15 biggest known bitcoin holders (data at 30 Oct 2026)

Definition: companies, funds and governments as listed by BitcoinTreasuries.net on 30 Oct 2026 (each fund counted on its own), plus Arkham's estimate for Satoshi Nakamoto. Not included: exchange and bank customer deposits (Xapo 38,931, River, Mt. Gox trustee), wrapped tokens (WBTC, cbBTC) and Ukraine's "holdings of public officials" (46,351, personal declarations). USD values at $78,400 per BTC (BitcoinTreasuries.net price on 30 Oct 2026).

Facts (BitcoinTreasuries.net entity pages, "as of" dates shown on each evidence card):
- 1 Satoshi Nakamoto: about 1.1 million BTC; Arkham attributes 1,096,361 BTC via the Patoshi pattern (The Crypto Basic, 26 May 2026). Estimate.
- 2 Strategy 848,517 (19 Oct 2026). 3 iShares Bitcoin Trust 811,115 (21 Oct 2026). 4 US government 328,372 (governments table; on-chain tracking, no official figure).
- 5 China 190,000 (estimate, PlusToken seizure). 6 Fidelity Wise Origin Bitcoin Fund 184,058 (25 Sep 2026). 7 Block.one 164,000 (14 Sep 2022, last reported).
- 8 Grayscale Bitcoin Trust 131,528 (21 Aug 2026). 9 Tether 100,000 (14 Aug 2026; Tether's Q2 2026 attestation showed 98,933). 10 Grayscale Bitcoin Mini Trust 62,874 (15 Sep 2026).
- 11 UK government 61,245. 12 Metaplanet 44,000 (5 Oct 2026). 13 Twenty One Capital 43,514 (30 Jul 2025, last reported). 14 Strive 43,000 (26 Oct 2026). 15 Bitwise Bitcoin ETF 38,189 (15 Sep 2026).
