Facebook: fb/ slideshow video from the 17 EN slides (social/_templates/slideshow, 2.3 s per slide). Telegram: one ranking chart (tg/, drawn by _source/rank_chart.py) plus the text below, one post per channel; EN text also goes to the Cambodia channel. **x** marks bold.

## facebook

Who owns the most bitcoin? We counted down the 15 biggest known holders, using BitcoinTreasuries.net data from 30 October.

At the top is still the person nobody can name: Satoshi Nakamoto, with an estimated 1.1 million BTC (Arkham estimate).

Next come Strategy with 848,517 BTC and BlackRock's iShares Bitcoin Trust with 811,115. Governments make the list too: the US with 328,372 and China with an estimated 190,000, both from on-chain tracking rather than official statements.

The rest are funds, companies and Tether, from Fidelity's FBTC at 184,058 down to Bitwise's BITB at 38,189. ETF coins are held for the funds' shareholders, and exchange customer deposits are not included.

Which name surprised you most?

Sources: BitcoinTreasuries.net, Arkham via The Crypto Basic. Photos: Wikimedia Commons, credited on each slide.
