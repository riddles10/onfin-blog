# More than $1 billion of crypto positions liquidated in 24 hours (8 to 9 Nov 2026)

Facts (verified 9 Nov 2026, about 08:30 UTC):
- crypto.news (9 Nov), citing CoinGlass: "more than $1 billion in cryptocurrency positions closed over a 24-hour period. Long positions ... accounted for approximately $931 million of the total. Ethereum recorded around $455 million in liquidations, compared with approximately $278 million for Bitcoin. The largest reported single liquidation involved an ETH position worth roughly $48 million." Bitcoin.com News gives $448M for ETH and $272M for BTC (an earlier CoinGlass snapshot); BeInCrypto via Yahoo gave $855.69M at an earlier point. Totals move as the 24-hour window rolls.
- The viral "$1 billion in 10 minutes" claim is not confirmed. Bitcoin.com News attributes "$1 billion in 10 to 15 minutes" to unnamed analysts; we use the CoinGlass 24-hour figure instead.
- Binance 1-minute candles (data-api.binance.vision), 8 Nov 2026, high at 21:40 UTC to low at 21:55 UTC: BTC $75,728.48 to $73,510.00 (-2.93%), ETH $2,226.04 to $2,060.00 (-7.46%), XRP $1.1464 to $1.0930 (-4.66%), SOL $89.10 to $85.02 (-4.58%). Other venues printed slightly different lows (BTC $73,477 per Bitcoin.com, $73,761 per crypto.news).
- Binance prices at 08:28 UTC on 9 Nov: BTC $74,212.87, ETH $2,109.70, XRP $1.0964, SOL $86.45.
- Context reported by crypto.news and Bitcoin.com News: US spot bitcoin ETFs had about $1.28 billion of net outflows from 2 to 6 Nov (Farside Investors); the drop came during thin weekend trading, alongside the Nvidia CEO news and stalled US and Iran talks. Neither outlet establishes how much of the move each factor caused, so the posts don't assign a cause.

## instagram

More than $1 billion of crypto positions were liquidated in 24 hours, according to CoinGlass data cited by crypto.news. About $931 million of that was long positions, bets that prices would rise.

The sharpest move came late on Sunday. Between 21:40 and 21:55 UTC on 8 November (04:40 to 04:55 on Monday, Cambodia time), Binance prices fell:
• BTC: $75,728 to $73,510 (2.9%)
• ETH: $2,226 to $2,060 (7.5%)
• XRP: $1.146 to $1.093 (4.7%)
• SOL: $89.10 to $85.02 (4.6%)

Ether took the biggest hit, with about $455 million liquidated against about $278 million for bitcoin. The largest single liquidation was an ETH position worth roughly $48 million.

Prices have bounced since, but not all the way: BTC was at $74,213 on Binance at 08:28 UTC on Monday.

A liquidation happens when a leveraged position runs out of collateral and the exchange closes it. When many close at once, that forced selling can push prices down even faster.

Sources: CoinGlass via crypto.news, Binance market data.

## facebook

In the early hours of Monday, Cambodia time, the crypto market dropped fast.

In just 15 minutes, from 04:40 to 04:55, bitcoin fell from $75,728 to $73,510 on Binance. Ether dropped 7.5% to $2,060, XRP slipped to $1.093 and Solana to $85.02.

Many traders were holding leveraged bets that prices would rise. When prices fall far enough, the exchange closes those positions automatically, which is called a liquidation. CoinGlass data cited by crypto.news shows more than $1 billion of positions liquidated over 24 hours, about $931 million of them long positions. Ether traders were hit hardest, with about $455 million liquidated, and the single biggest liquidation was an ETH position worth roughly $48 million.

Prices have bounced since, but not all the way back. Bitcoin was at $74,213 on Binance at 15:28 Cambodia time.

Moves like this are why position size and stop losses matter, especially with leverage. Would your stop loss have survived that candle?
