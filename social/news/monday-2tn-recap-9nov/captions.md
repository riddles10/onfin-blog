# Fact check: "$2 trillion added to the US stock market today; everything green except Nvidia" (Mon 9 Nov 2026)

Claim (a Telegram trading channel, posted at the 9 Nov close): "$2 TRILLION has been added to the US stock market today. Literally everything is green, well except for NVIDIA." The image showed NVDA at -5.19%.

Verdict: close, but not exact. About $1.8 trillion at the close (it touched about $2 trillion in the afternoon), Nvidia closed down 4.27% not 5.19%, and not everything was green.

Facts (checked 10 Nov 2026, about 15:15 UTC):
- Yahoo Finance daily closes, Mon 9 Nov vs Fri 6 Nov: S&P 500 7,549.94 (+2.35%), Nasdaq Composite 25,253.14 (+2.74%), Dow 52,389.75 (+2.15%). Nvidia $178.67 (-4.27%, from $186.64).
- CNN (9 Nov): S&P 500 best day since April, Nasdaq best since July; Nvidia "fell as much as 8.8% shortly after Monday's opening bell before paring some declines and ending the day down roughly 4.3%. The drop erased roughly $192 billion from Nvidia's market value."
- Value gained: 2.35% of about $76.2 trillion, the US market's value at Friday's close (Siblis Research $78.74 trillion on 30 Sep 2026, scaled by the Wilshire 5000), about $1.79 trillion (OnFin estimate). Our 9 Nov fact check put the gain at about $2.0 trillion at 20:20 UTC; it faded into the close.
- Of 25 of the largest US stocks (Yahoo Finance), two besides Nvidia fell: Visa -0.12% and Exxon Mobil -0.69%. Also lower on the day: Axon -3.14%, Hims & Hers -11.77%. The -5.19% for Nvidia in the viral image was an intraday reading, not the close.

But "everything green" isn't quite right: of the 25 largest US stocks we checked, Visa and Exxon Mobil also closed lower (Yahoo Finance).
