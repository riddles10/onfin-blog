## facebook

Nvidia says it will put $3 billion into Meta's new share sale. The company that sells Meta its AI chips is set to become a Meta shareholder (NVIDIA newsroom, 26 October).

The headline leaves out the scale. Meta priced $25 billion of new stock at $625 a share plus $12.5 billion of convertible preferred shares, about $37.5 billion in all (Meta). Nvidia's part is about 8% of that.

There is fine print too. Meta's filing says the interest from Nvidia, AMD and Broadcom, up to $5 billion combined, is not binding. AMD has since said it will put in $1 billion (AMD).

And the money flows both ways: Meta plans to deploy more Nvidia hardware, including Vera Rubin systems (NVIDIA). Meta shares closed at $624.52 on 29 October, down 13.88% since 30 September (Yahoo Finance).

Smart business or circular money?

Sources: NVIDIA, Meta, AMD, Yahoo Finance. Video: 24/7 Wall St via Yahoo Finance.
