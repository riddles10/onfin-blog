## instagram

Nvidia says it will put $3 billion into Meta's share sale. The company that sells Meta its AI chips is set to become a Meta shareholder (NVIDIA newsroom, 26 October).

The headline leaves out the scale. Meta priced $25 billion of new common stock at $625 a share plus $12.5 billion of convertible preferred shares, about $37.5 billion in all (Meta). Nvidia's $3 billion is about 8% of that.

The fine print: Meta's filing says the interest from Nvidia, AMD and Broadcom, up to $5 billion combined, is not binding. AMD has since said it will put in $1 billion (AMD).

The money also flows back. Meta plans to deploy more Nvidia hardware, including Vera Rubin systems (NVIDIA). Jensen Huang called AI agents "a new computing paradigm". Mark Zuckerberg said: "Muse is how we bring personal superintelligence to everyone."

Meta shares closed at $624.52 on 29 October, down 13.88% since 30 September (Yahoo Finance).
