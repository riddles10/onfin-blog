# Fact check: "$1 trillion added to the US stock market at open" (Mon 9 Nov 2026)

Claim (Watcher.Guru on Facebook): "$1,000,000,000,000 added to the US stock market at open."

Verdict: roughly right, but it took about 10 minutes, not the opening tick.

Facts (checked 9 Nov 2026, 20:15 to 20:25 UTC):
- S&P 500, Yahoo Finance 1-minute data: Friday 6 Nov close 7,376.86. First print on Monday 7,422.24 at 14:30 UTC (+0.62%). 7,478.85 at 14:40 UTC (+1.38%). 7,571.26 at 20:20 UTC (+2.64%), with the market still open.
- US stock market value: Siblis Research puts all US-listed stocks at $78.74 trillion on 30 Sep 2026. Scaled by the Wilshire 5000 (76,039.05 on 30 Sep, 73,564.76 on 6 Nov), that is about $76.2 trillion at Friday's close (OnFin estimate). Using the S&P 500 move for the whole market: +0.62% is about $0.47 trillion, +1.38% about $1.05 trillion, and +2.64% about $2.0 trillion.
- Why (JournalArta, 9 Nov): Brent fell 5.02% to $93.38 and WTI 4.36% to $86.87 "as hopes grew for a diplomatic end to the Iran war"; AMD jumped 11.04% early. Yahoo Finance live blog: Nvidia fell as much as 8% after its board opened a probe into its CEO; economists surveyed by Bloomberg expect October CPI at +0.3% month on month.
- Coinpaper and Yahoo Finance report similar opening moves (S&P 500 +0.81% to +0.9% at the bell).
