## facebook

On 29 October, Alphabet lost about $500 billion in market value. The BBC's headline: Google's own AI search was hitting its ad sales.

What did Google actually say? On its earnings call the day before, search revenue was up 12% to $63 billion. Chief business officer Philipp Schindler said currency moves were the single largest reason growth slowed from the previous quarter.

AI was part of it too. Strong growth in AI Mode queries "had an impact because of the lower monetization we were seeing in AI Mode," Schindler said. Google says it is now scaling ads inside AI Overviews and AI Mode.

The shares closed at $279.84 on 30 October, their lowest close since 30 March and 30.5% below the 13 May close (Yahoo Finance data).
