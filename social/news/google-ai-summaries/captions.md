# Is Google's own AI eating its ad business? (follow-up to the 29 Oct worst day)

Facts:
- BBC News (Kali Hays and Mitchell Labiak, 30 Oct 2026, "Google value plunges by $500bn as its own AI search hits ad sales"): Alphabet lost just over $500bn in value on Thursday 29 Oct "after it revealed its own artificial intelligence (AI) summaries were hitting search advertising revenue"; shares fell over 12% to a six-month low; market value $3.97tn to $3.46tn. Kim Scott, who formerly led Google's advertising business (per BBC), on the Today programme: Google would "rather eat its own lunch than allow someone else to eat their lunch". Tracy Woo (Forrester): the Search results "highlights the challenge of funding massive AI investments while maintaining the strength of established businesses."
- Alphabet Q3 2026 earnings call, 28 Oct 2026 (transcript: The Singju Post):
  - Philipp Schindler (Chief Business Officer): "Search grew 12% year over year to 63 billion"; on the slowdown from Q2: "So first, there was FX. It was the single largest driver of Search deceleration relative to Q2." Second, vertical dynamics (EU de minimis change, World Cup timing). Then: "the strong growth in AI Mode queries had an impact because of the lower monetization we were seeing in AI Mode. To be clear, we feel very good about our trajectory here." Google is "now scaling ads within both AI Overviews and AI Mode".
  - Doug Anmuth (JPMorgan) asked whether Google "haven't been showing as many ads basically in AI Overviews in AI Mode".
  - Sundar Pichai (CEO): "our year over year query growth rate is the highest it's been in the last five years."
  - Anat Ashkenazi (CFO): "Google Search and other advertising revenues increased by 12% to $63.3 billion".
- Six-month-low check (Yahoo Finance daily data): GOOGL closed $283.82 on 29 Oct and $279.84 on 30 Oct, the lowest closes since 30 Mar 2026 ($273.50), so about seven months; BBC's "six-month low" is broadly right. 30 Oct close is 30.5% below the 13 May 2026 close of $402.62.
- Framing note: Google's own words make AI Mode one factor, not the main one. Currency was the largest driver of the slowdown. The slides and captions say this.
