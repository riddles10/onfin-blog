# Binance hack, 29 Oct 2026 (developing story)

Figures as of 30 Oct 2026, about 02:00 UTC:
- Chainalysis: about $5.912 billion stolen at the time of theft; final loss undetermined (via Cointelegraph live, 30 Oct 00:26 UTC). CoinDesk calls it the biggest crypto hack in history.
- Binance: deposits and withdrawals temporarily suspended; "Binance will cover all losses" (Binance and co-CEO Yi He on X); verified frozen total raised to $3 billion from $2.54 billion (Cointelegraph, 30 Oct 00:52 UTC). Tether froze over $1.5 billion USDT, Circle over $800 million USDC.
- Binance initial findings (CoinDesk live, 29 Oct 10:36 pm EDT): compromised DevOps engineer laptop; "We have found no evidence of any wallet private key leak." Full post-mortem to come.
- Binance.US statement, 29 Oct: not affected.
- Prices (Cointelegraph, 30 Oct 01:19 UTC): BTC about $76,937 (-4.9% 24h), ETH about $2,211 (-5.7%), BNB about $693 (-8%).

## instagram

Binance was hit by what Chainalysis estimates is the largest crypto hack on record: about $5.9 billion at the time of the theft.

Binance has paused deposits and withdrawals and says it will cover all losses. It says about $3 billion has been frozen so far, with help from Tether, Circle and others. Binance.US says it is not affected.

Binance's early findings, reported by CoinDesk, point to a compromised staff laptop, and Binance says it found no evidence of leaked wallet keys. A full post-mortem is still to come, and the final loss is not yet confirmed.

Bitcoin fell about 5% and BNB about 8% in 24 hours (Cointelegraph, 30 Oct).

Scammers often follow hacks. Ignore refund or recovery links, and never share your seed phrase or 2FA codes.

Sources: Chainalysis, Binance, Binance.US, CoinDesk, Cointelegraph. Developing story, figures as of 30 Oct, 02:00 UTC.
