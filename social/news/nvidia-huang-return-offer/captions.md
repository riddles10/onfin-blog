# Jensen Huang says he would return as Nvidia CEO if the board asks (17 Nov 2026)

Facts (checked 17 Nov 2026, about 19:30 UTC):
- Huang's post on X, 17 Nov (full text via TechCrunch): "If the Board of Directors decides that my return is in the best interest of our shareholders, our employees, the company, and America, I would be honored to answer these calls and return as CEO." Also: "I did not live up to the standards expected of everyone at NVIDIA, and I have made serious mistakes in my personal life. I created problems for the Board that I deeply regret. I own my mistakes completely, and I am committed to cooperating with the Board's independent investigation."
- Board: a person familiar told CNBC on Monday the board isn't considering bringing him back while it runs a national security investigation (Fortune); a board source told The Washington Post there were no plans to reinstate him (TechCrunch).
- Support: an open letter with more than 170,000 signatures (affiliations unverified), backed by Travis Kalanick, Tim Sweeney and Garry Tan; Elon Musk said he is signing; Trump said he "would not mind" Huang returning (Fortune).
- Nvidia has lost about $450 billion in market value since he admitted the affair (Fortune). CFO Colette Kress is interim CEO.
- Today, 17 Nov: Nvidia's board set up a National Security and Export Control Oversight Committee (Nvidia). Q3 results are due at about 1:20 p.m. PT (21:20 UTC), the first without Huang (Nvidia, Fortune). Huang sits for a closed-door House interview on 1 Dec (Fortune).
- Price (Yahoo Finance): $153.99 at 19:23 UTC on 17 Nov, -1.4% on the day.
