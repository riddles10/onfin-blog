## instagram

The US economy grew at a 3.4% annual rate in the third quarter, up from 2.2% in the second, according to the Bureau of Economic Analysis advance estimate released on 29 October.

That is the fastest pace since the third quarter of 2025, and it beat the 3.1% economists had forecast (BEA, Yahoo Finance).

Consumer spending rose at a 3.8% pace and business investment excluding housing jumped 12.7%, with information processing equipment up 37.4% as the AI build-out continues. Housing investment fell 3.3% (AP).

Inflation cooled in the quarter: the PCE price index rose 2.1%, down from 5.0% in the second quarter (BEA).

This is the first of three estimates. The second is due on 25 November, and early GDP figures are often revised. The report came out five days before the 3 November midterm elections.

Sources: BEA, AP, Yahoo Finance. Photo: Architect of the Capitol, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons.
