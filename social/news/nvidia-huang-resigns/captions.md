# Breaking news: Jensen Huang resigns as Nvidia CEO (12 Nov 2026)

Facts (Nvidia release "NVIDIA Announces CEO Transition", 12 Nov 2026):
- "Jensen Huang has resigned as President, Chief Executive Officer, and a member of the Board, effective immediately."
- Colette Kress, EVP and CFO since September 2013, is interim CEO and keeps the CFO role until a permanent successor is named; the board will consider internal and external candidates.
- Harvey C. Jones is appointed the board's first-ever Chair.
- Kress: "I am committed to building on the extraordinary foundation Jensen created over the last 33 years."
- Q3 fiscal 2027 results still due Tue 17 Nov, about 1:20 p.m. PT.
- The release gives no reason. Context: the board's 8 Nov investigation into reports that Huang was "the target of a foreign intelligence effort" (Nvidia); NYT report of a relationship with an executive who reported to him (via Yahoo Finance).
- Price (Yahoo Finance): opened at $149.29 (-14.1%); $153.46 at 14:32 UTC on 12 Nov, -11.7% vs Wednesday's close of $173.73 (the badge figure).

## instagram

Breaking news: Jensen Huang has resigned as Nvidia's CEO, president and board member, effective immediately, the company announced on 12 November.

CFO Colette Kress will serve as interim CEO while keeping her CFO role, and the board will consider internal and external candidates for a permanent successor. Harvey C. Jones becomes Nvidia's first-ever board chair.

"I am committed to building on the extraordinary foundation Jensen created over the last 33 years," Kress said. Nvidia gave no reason for his departure. It comes days after the board opened an investigation following reports that Huang had been the target of a foreign intelligence effort, and a New York Times report that he had a relationship with an executive who reported to him.

Nvidia shares fell about 12% in early trading: $153.46 at 14:32 UTC, against Wednesday's close of $173.73 (Yahoo Finance). Third-quarter results are still due on 17 November.
