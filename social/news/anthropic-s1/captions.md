# Anthropic files its S-1 for an IPO, 26 Oct 2026

Facts (confirmed in primary sources):
- Anthropic announcement, 26 Oct 2026: Anthropic, PBC filed a registration statement on Form S-1 with the SEC for a proposed IPO of Class A common stock, to list on the Nasdaq Global Select Market as "ANT". Share count and price range not yet determined; the registration statement is not yet effective. A confidential draft S-1 was submitted in June 2026 (NPR, 1 Jun 2026).
- S-1 (sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2130357/000162828026067168/anthropicpbc-sx1.htm):
  - revenue $16.3B in the six months to 30 Jun 2026, +1,300% YoY; 2025 revenue $4.6B
  - net loss $80.7B for the six months to 30 Jun 2026 (2025: $42.0B); it includes $72.4B of change in fair value of convertible notes, derivative liabilities and SAFEs
  - operating income $974M in Q2 2026, after an operating loss of $1,667M in Q1 2026
  - customers with $100,000+ TTM revenue: about 20 (31 Dec 2023), about 200 (2024), about 1,500 (2025), more than 11,900 (30 Sep 2026)
  - risk factor: advanced AI systems could "pose catastrophic or existential risks to humanity"
  - governance: the LTBT (Class T stock) elects the Class T directors, a board majority; "the LTBT and the Founder LLC will collectively have the ability to elect all members of our board", and the Founder LLC can determine the outcome of stockholder votes "for the foreseeable future"
  - underwriters on the cover start with Goldman Sachs & Co. LLC and Morgan Stanley.
- Yahoo Finance (Daniel Howley, 26 Oct 2026): $16.3B vs $4.6B; "the vast majority" of the $80.7B loss "was the result of accounting charges".
- Yahoo Finance video, Brian Sozzi, 26 Oct 2026 ("3 quick takeaways from Anthropic's IPO filing"): used 56.7 to 62.2 s and 73.85 to 85.4 s ("keyword, humanity").

Reported, not confirmed in the filing:
- Valuation: NBC News, the offering "could value the Claude developer at more than $2 trillion". The S-1 has no valuation, price or share count.
- Timing: trading could start as soon as 16 Nov (Yahoo Finance headline); marketing from the week of 9 Nov (Bloomberg, via secondary sources).
- Michael Burry (Business Insider via Yahoo Finance, 1 Jun 2026): Anthropic "might never be worth $1 trillion as he expects demand to cool". This is from June, before the public filing.

## instagram

Anthropic, the company behind Claude, has filed to go public. Its S-1 went to the SEC on 26 October, for Class A shares on Nasdaq under the ticker ANT. The share count and price range are not set yet.

What the filing shows (Anthropic S-1):
Revenue of $16.3 billion in the six months to June, against $4.6 billion in all of 2025.
A net loss of $80.7 billion over the same six months, of which $72.4 billion came from changes in the fair value of convertible notes and similar financing instruments.
Operating income of $974 million in the second quarter.
Customers paying $100,000 or more a year: about 20 at the end of 2023, more than 11,900 by 30 September 2026.

The filing also warns that advanced AI could "pose catastrophic or existential risks to humanity", and says a trust and the founders are expected to elect the whole board.

Reports say the listing could value Anthropic at more than $2 trillion (NBC News). That figure is not in the filing. In June, Michael Burry said Anthropic might never be worth $1 trillion (Business Insider).

Sources: Anthropic, Anthropic S-1 (SEC), Yahoo Finance, NBC News, Business Insider. Video: Yahoo Finance. Photo: TechCrunch, CC BY 2.0, via Wikimedia Commons.
