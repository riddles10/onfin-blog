## facebook

On 22 October, results from Moderna and Merck's melanoma vaccine trial leaked online, two days before their planned debut at the ESMO cancer conference (Bloomberg).

The data was good. Given with Merck's Keytruda, the vaccine cut the risk of the cancer returning or death by 42% versus Keytruda alone (Merck and Moderna).

The stock still fell. Moderna closed at $202.63, down 17.57%, and at its low was down 26%, its biggest intraday drop on record (The Motley Fool, Yahoo Finance).

Why? An earlier, smaller trial had shown 49%, and the shares had nearly quadrupled since mid-August (Yahoo Finance). Investors had priced in more.

Have you ever seen good news send a stock lower?

Sources: Bloomberg, Yahoo Finance, The Motley Fool, Merck and Moderna. Video: ABC News Live.
