## instagram

Moderna shares closed down 17.57% at $202.63 on 22 October, after results from its personalized melanoma vaccine trial leaked two days before their planned debut at the ESMO cancer conference (The Motley Fool, Bloomberg).

The data itself was positive: given with Merck's Keytruda, the vaccine cut the risk of the cancer returning or death by 42% versus Keytruda alone (Merck and Moderna). An earlier, smaller trial had shown 49%, and the stock had nearly quadrupled since mid-August (Yahoo Finance).

At its low the stock was down 26%, its biggest intraday drop on record (Yahoo Finance). It closed at $222.76 on 29 October (Yahoo Finance data).
