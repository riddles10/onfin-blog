# Fact check: are half of Nvidia's employees worth $25M+?

Claim (Polymarket/coingrams post, about 26 Oct 2026): "Half of Nvidia employees are worth $25M+", and "Nvidia has lost roughly $750 billion since early October, falling 13% from its record high".

Verdict:
- $25M figure: not new and unverified. It traces to The Kobeissi Letter on X, 4 Aug 2025 ("Roughly 50% of Nvidia employees are now worth over $25 million. Roughly 80% of Nvidia employees are now millionaires."). Benzinga (Ananya Gairola, 5 Aug 2025, on Yahoo Finance): based on "a June survey of more than 3,000 Nvidia employees", roughly 10% of a 30,000-person workforce; 76-78% of respondents millionaires, nearly half reporting net worth above $25M. The survey's organiser and method are not published; figures are self-reported; Nvidia has not published it.
- Huang quote: "I've created more billionaires on my management team than any CEO in the world" (podcast with Chamath Palihapitiya and Jason Calacanis, July 2025, via Benzinga).
- Stock: Yahoo Finance daily data. Record close $239.24 on 6 Oct 2026 (intraday high $243.37 the same day). Close $210.96 on 23 Oct (13.3% below the intraday record), $213.66 on 29 Oct (10.7% below the record close). The "13%" matches the 23 Oct low; Gotrade put market value at $5.16T at $214.13 on 16 Oct, which implies roughly $750B-$780B off the peak at the low.

## instagram

Are half of Nvidia's employees really worth more than $25 million? The figure is going around again, but it is not new and it is not an official number.

It traces to an August 2025 post on X by The Kobeissi Letter: "Roughly 50% of Nvidia employees are now worth over $25 million" (Benzinga via Yahoo Finance, 5 August 2025).

Behind it is a June 2025 survey of more than 3,000 employees, about 10% of Nvidia's workforce at the time. 76% to 78% of respondents said they were millionaires, and nearly half reported a net worth above $25 million. Nvidia has not published the survey, and the figures are self-reported (Benzinga).

Jensen Huang has made a similar point about his executives: "I've created more billionaires on my management team than any CEO in the world" (July 2025, via Benzinga).

Most of that wealth is Nvidia stock, so it moves with the share price. Nvidia closed at a record $239.24 on 6 October and at $213.66 on 29 October, down 10.7%. At the 23 October close it was about 13% below its intraday record of $243.37 (Yahoo Finance data).

Sources: The Kobeissi Letter, Benzinga, Yahoo Finance. Photo: Office of the President (Taiwan), CC BY 2.0, via Wikimedia Commons.
