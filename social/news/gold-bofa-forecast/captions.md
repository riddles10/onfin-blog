# Gold: Bank of America says it can't hold $4,000, for now (1 Nov 2026)

Facts:
- Gold (COMEX futures, GC=F, Yahoo Finance daily closes): record close $5,318.40 on 29 Jan 2026 (intraday high $5,586.20 that day). Close on 30 Oct 2026: $3,898.90, down 3.3% on the day, 26.7% below the record close, down 6.9% in October and 9.9% this year.
- Bank of America, metals note published Monday 12 Oct 2026 (Kitco News, Neils Christensen, 12 Oct): analysts expect gold to be "unable to hold above $4,000 an ounce over the next three months" (Kitco's wording). Quotes from the note: "We remain cautious near-term as the macro environment remains challenging: we have an average 4Q26 forecast of $4,000/oz, but then expect prices to average $4,813 in CY27." "Our tracker suggests that other key market participants, including central banks, China and India, have, in aggregate, slowed gold buying." Gold "will in all likelihood only bottom out once concerns over inflation/the Iran war subside, USD stops appreciating, and the Fed is no longer in a tightening cycle." No analyst is named in the coverage.
- Bank of America outlook of 30 Sep 2026 (investingLive, Eamonn Sheridan, 1 Oct): Q4 2026 average $4,000 with a risk of dips toward $3,750; $5,000 averages kept for Q2 and Q3 2027; an oil spike is "the biggest threat" to the forecast; $150 oil (not the bank's base case) would cut the 2027 average to about $3,500.
- Goldman Sachs (investingLive, 23 Sep 2026): end-2026 fair value cut to $4,650 from $4,900; $5,400 target for end-2027 kept.
- So BofA's call is a near-term bearish view inside a longer-term higher forecast, not a call for a lasting collapse. The user's summary ("gold could decline further") matches the near-term part.

## instagram

Gold closed at $3,898.90 an ounce on 30 October, below $4,000 and 26.7% under its record close of $5,318.40 in January (COMEX gold futures, Yahoo Finance).

That is in line with Bank of America's latest view. In a note published on 12 October, its analysts said they "remain cautious near-term" and expect gold to be unable to hold above $4,000 over the next three months (Kitco News).

Their fourth-quarter forecast is a $4,000 average, with a risk of dips toward $3,750 (investingLive, on BofA's 30 September outlook). The biggest threat they flag is an oil spike tied to the Iran war, and they note that central banks, China and India have slowed their gold buying.

It is not a long-term bearish call: BofA still expects gold to average $4,813 in 2027. Goldman Sachs is more upbeat, with a $5,400 target for end-2027 (investingLive, 23 September).
