# Jensen Huang admits an affair; Nvidia's board opens a probe (8 to 9 Nov 2026)

Facts (verified 9 Nov 2026, 08:00 to 09:00 UTC):
- Nvidia Newsroom, "Statement From NVIDIA Board of Directors", 8 Nov 2026: "The NVIDIA Board is initiating an independent investigation in light of recent reporting that founder and CEO Jensen Huang has been the target of a foreign intelligence effort. A committee of independent directors, assisted by outside independent counsel, will oversee the investigation. ... While the Board has no reason to believe that either national security or NVIDIA secrets were compromised, the Board's inquiry will examine all available information. Jensen fully supports the review and has committed to cooperating with it. The Board has confidence in Jensen and his leadership team."
- Huang's memo to staff, which Nvidia posted publicly, quoted by SF Standard (8 Nov): "I owe my family and all of you an apology. ... I was unfaithful to my wife. My actions hurt her and our children. There is no excuse for what I did. I am deeply sorry." The memo does not name the woman or address the spying reports.
- SF Standard, citing The New York Times: Huang told the board the relationship was with an executive at Megaspeed, an Nvidia customer, whom US intelligence officials concluded was working for the Chinese government. The FT previously reported US intelligence believed the two were in a romantic relationship (CNN). These are reports citing officials, not findings. We don't name her.
- CNN (8 to 9 Nov): China's foreign ministry spokesperson Guo Jiakun called the claim "purely groundless speculation and an attempt to smear and malign China". Nvidia spokesperson: "Jensen is committed to continuing to lead NVIDIA as CEO without interruption." Dan Ives (Yorkville & Ives): "a black eye moment for Jensen".
- CNBC (8 Nov): three House Republican committee chairs asked Huang for a closed-door briefing on 18 Nov; Nvidia reports Q3 results on 17 Nov.
- Reuters via The Kathmandu Post (9 Nov): Nvidia at about $180.30 in out-of-hours trading on Monday (Hyperliquid perpetuals, Blue Ocean), down about 3.4% from Friday's Nasdaq close of $186.64.
- Yahoo Finance daily closes: high $239.24 on 6 Oct 2026; $186.64 on 6 Nov, which is 22.0% lower. Weekly closes fell five weeks in a row from $233.95 on 2 Oct (also reported by Gulf News).

## facebook

On Sunday, Nvidia's CEO Jensen Huang sent his staff an unusual memo. "I owe my family and all of you an apology," he wrote. "I was unfaithful to my wife."

Hours later, Nvidia's board announced an independent investigation, not into the affair itself but into reports that Huang had been "the target of a foreign intelligence effort". According to The New York Times, the relationship was with an executive at an Nvidia customer whom US intelligence officials believed was working for the Chinese government. China's foreign ministry called the claim "purely groundless speculation".

The board says it has "no reason to believe that either national security or NVIDIA secrets were compromised", and that it has confidence in Huang. A committee of independent directors, with outside counsel, will run the inquiry.

Investors reacted before Wall Street opened. Nvidia traded at about $180.30 out of hours on Monday, around 3.4% below Friday's close (Reuters). The stock was already 22% below its October high after five straight weekly losses, and third-quarter results are due on 17 November.
