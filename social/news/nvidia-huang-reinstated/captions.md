# Breaking news: Jensen Huang reinstated as Nvidia CEO (23 Nov 2026)

Facts (checked 24 Nov 2026, 05:20 UTC):
- Nvidia press release, 23 Nov: "effective immediately, Jensen Huang has been reinstated as President and Chief Executive Officer and as a member of the Board. The Board of Directors voted unanimously to reinstate Mr. Huang ... the Board received input from senior levels of the United States government, and based on that input, the Board is confident that Mr. Huang's reinstatement poses no national security risk." Harvey Jones stays as chair; Kress returns to CFO. The independent committee's investigation "will proceed to conclusion". Nvidia is hiring a Chief Compliance Officer.
- Huang (release): "I am honored to return to NVIDIA ... I take this responsibility with the greatest humility and renewed commitment."
- NPR, 23 Nov: the board said as recently as 17 Nov that it did not intend to reinstate him. Trump to CNBC: "I spoke to them. Let me just put it that way. And I convinced them." Commerce added Megaspeed to its restricted list; the Justice Department is investigating Megaspeed for possible chip smuggling.
- Prices (Yahoo Finance closes): $186.64 on 6 Nov, $125.53 on Fri 20 Nov, $165.05 on Mon 23 Nov (+31.5%).
