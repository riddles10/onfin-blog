## facebook

US Thanksgiving trading schedule

US markets close for Thanksgiving on Thursday 26 November and close early on Friday 27 November. These are OnFin's adjusted closing times, in trading server time (EET, UTC+2). Cambodia is 5 hours ahead, so 20:00 server time is 01:00 the next morning in Cambodia.

Thursday 26 November
• S&P 500, Nasdaq 100, Dow Jones 30, Nikkei 225, Russell 2000: 20:00
• ASX 200, Euro Stoxx 50, CAC 40, DAX 40, FTSE 100: 23:00
• Metals, WTI: 21:30
• NGAS, Brent: 20:30
• HK50: 21:00
• US share CFDs: closed

Friday 27 November
• S&P 500, Nasdaq 100, Dow Jones 30, Nikkei 225, Russell 2000: 20:15
• ASX 200, Euro Stoxx 50, CAC 40, DAX 40, FTSE 100: 23:00
• Metals, WTI: 21:45
• Brent: 22:00
• NGAS: 20:30
• HK50: 21:00
• US share CFDs: 20:00

With many US traders away, liquidity thins out around the holiday. Expect wider spreads and more slippage.

How to reduce slippage: https://blog.onfin.io/en/what-is-slippage-in-forex-and-how-to-avoid-it

Not investment advice. CFDs are high-risk; you may lose money.
