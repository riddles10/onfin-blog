## instagram

Microsoft is offering a Voluntary Exit Offer to about 60,000 employees, roughly 27% of its global workforce (AP).

The offer, announced on 26 October by chief people officer Amy Coleman, covers participating teams in Canada, India, the UK and the US at level 70 and below. Microsoft called it "new for us" (Microsoft blog).

It follows about 4,800 job cuts in July, around 2.1% of staff, and a voluntary retirement programme that more than 30% of eligible employees took up (Microsoft blog). Microsoft had about 223,000 full-time employees at the end of June (AP).

Seattle University economist Joseph Philips told KIRO 7 the company has "continued to hire people while they're firing people." Microsoft shares rose about 1% on the day of the announcement (AP).

Sources: Microsoft, AP, KIRO 7. Photo: Coolcaesar, CC BY-SA 4.0, via Wikimedia Commons.
