Check (12 Nov 2026, about 13:00 UTC):
- The WSJ did report that the board was preparing to oust Huang. The second-affair allegation comes from The New York Times, not the WSJ.
- The board met late on Wednesday (Reuters via TNW). Yahoo Finance's live blog: "There was still no word Thursday morning on the outcome of an Nvidia board meeting held Wednesday to determine Jensen Huang's future."
