Title: Binance says 95% of hacked funds are now covered

Three days after the biggest crypto hack on record, Binance says it has covered almost all of it.

About $6.3 billion in tokens was stolen from Binance's hot wallets last week (CNBC). On 31 Oct Binance said 90% had been replenished, up from about 80% a day earlier. Late that evening it put the figure at 95%, Reuters reports. The gap is in less liquid tokens it is still buying.

The money comes from Binance itself: its corporate treasury, part of its SAFU emergency fund and part of its futures insurance fund. "Binance will absorb the financial impact of the stolen funds, not users," a spokesperson told Cointelegraph.

Binance said it would restart withdrawals in phases on 1 Nov, from 06:00 to 09:00 UTC, starting with bitcoin, ether, BNB and other major tokens (Reuters).

Two things are still open. CertiK said it needed more information to verify the replacement funds, and the proof behind Binance's new reserves snapshot takes 40 to 50 hours to generate (Cointelegraph). The FBI has linked the attack to North Korea's TraderTraitor unit (Reuters).

Scammers often follow hacks. Never share your password, 2FA codes or seed phrase with anyone who contacts you.
