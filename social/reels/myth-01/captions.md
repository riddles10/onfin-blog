## EN (Instagram adds the hashtag line; Facebook and Telegram without it)

Myth #01: lose 50%, then gain 50%, and you're back to even.

Fact: you need a 100% gain to recover.

Hypothetical example: start with $1,000. Lose 50% and you have $500. A 50% gain on $500 only gets you to $750. To get back to $1,000, you need +100%.

The bigger the loss, the steeper the climb: -10% needs +11%, -25% needs +33%, -50% needs +100%, -75% needs +300%.
