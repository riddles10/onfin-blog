## instagram

Weekly giveaway for Cambodia: win $50 USDT.

This week's question: where will gold (XAU/USD) be on Tuesday 3 November at 20:00 Cambodia time?

How to enter:
1. Follow @onfinkm
2. Comment your price prediction, for example 4052.75
3. Enter by Sunday 1 November, 23:59 Cambodia time

The closest prediction wins $50 USDT. We'll announce the winner on Thursday 5 November.

This is the same giveaway as our reel. Comments on this post or the reel both count, one entry per person.

Rules:
• Open to residents of Cambodia aged 18 or over. OnFin staff and their families can't enter.
• The giveaway runs on our Instagram and Facebook, with one winner in total.
• One entry per person across Instagram and Facebook. Only your first comment counts, and it must contain one price.
• Entries close Sunday 1 November at 23:59 Cambodia time (16:59 UTC), before the market reopens.
• The result is the XAU/USD bid price on OnFin MT5 at 20:00 Cambodia time (13:00 UTC) on Tuesday 3 November, taken from the opening price of that one-minute candle. We'll post a screenshot.
• If two entries are equally close, the earlier comment wins.
• We'll reply to the winning comment and message the winner from @onfinkm. The winner has 72 hours to reply, confirm they live in Cambodia and share a USDT (TRC-20) wallet address. If they don't, the next closest eligible entry wins.
• We pay within 7 days of confirmation. No cash alternative.
• We only contact winners from @onfinkm on Instagram or the OnFin Cambodia page on Facebook. We will never ask for a payment, a deposit, your password or card details.
• No purchase, account or deposit needed.
