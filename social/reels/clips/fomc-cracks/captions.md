Can higher rates slow an AI investment boom? A popular view says no, because investors expect returns that are too big to care about borrowing costs.

Fed chair Kevin Warsh disagrees: "Interest rates get in the cracks." He said rate moves since September have already shown up in what investors choose to fund.

When borrowing costs change, they reach almost every market, tech stocks included.
