# Clip reel: Ray Dalio, broke in 1982

Source: Lex Fridman Podcast #54 with Ray Dalio, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=M95m2EFb7IQ (22:08 to 22:14, 22:19 to 22:43 and 22:43 to 22:55).

## instagram

In 1982 Ray Dalio publicly predicted a depression. It turned out to be close to the exact bottom of the stock market.

He lost money for himself and his clients, let his staff go, and borrowed $4,000 from his dad to pay family bills.

He calls it one of the best things that ever happened to him, because it changed how he makes decisions. He went on to build Bridgewater Associates.
