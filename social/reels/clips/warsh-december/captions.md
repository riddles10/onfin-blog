# Clip reel: Warsh on why the Fed didn't hike in October

Source: Federal Reserve press conference, 28 October 2026, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hs8YWtNQQ40 (7:00 to 7:06 and 7:07 to 7:09 and 7:30 to 7:51).

28 October 2026. The Fed had just held rates at 3.75 to 4 percent. A reporter said most of Kevin Warsh's colleagues seemed ready to raise rates in December and asked: why not just raise them today?

His answer: beating inflation "is not about some fixed individual date on a calendar," and "it's not about some isolated data point." He pointed to "nearly six years of inflation above target."

The next Fed decision is due on 9 December (Federal Reserve). Before that, US inflation data for October is out on 10 November (BLS).
