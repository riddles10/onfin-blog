# Clip reel: Powell at Jackson Hole 2025: "may warrant adjusting"

Source: Jackson Hole Economic Policy Symposium, 22 August 2025, remarks by Fed chair Jerome Powell, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ae-ByiDOS10 (9:12 to 9:26 and 9:49 to 9:58).

## instagram

One sentence from Jerome Powell at Jackson Hole on 22 August 2025: the shifting balance of risks "may warrant adjusting our policy stance."

Markets read it as a signal of a September rate cut. The Dow rose 846 points, about 1.9 percent, to a record close that day, and the S&P 500 gained 1.5 percent (AP, Nasdaq).

Central bank wording can move markets in minutes, in either direction.
