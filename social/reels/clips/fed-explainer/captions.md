# Clip reel: How the Fed moves interest rates

Source: Fed Functions: Conducting Monetary Policy, Federal Reserve, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xHQJBNO0yQc (1:23 to 1:57).

## instagram

The Fed has one main lever, the federal funds rate, which is the rate for overnight borrowing between banks.

Lower it, and borrowing gets cheaper and asset prices tend to get a lift. Raise it, and borrowing costs rise across the economy.

That is why traders watch every FOMC meeting. The next one is on 8 and 9 December 2026.
