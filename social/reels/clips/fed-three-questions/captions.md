# Clip reel: The three questions the FOMC asks

Source: Fed Functions: Conducting Monetary Policy, Federal Reserve, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xHQJBNO0yQc (2:16 to 2:46).

## instagram

Before every rate decision, the FOMC weighs three questions, according to the Fed's own explainer: how the US economy is likely to evolve, what the right policy setting is, and how to explain it to the public.

To answer them, policymakers look at the latest economic data, plus reports and surveys from businesses and financial market contacts.

That is why data days can move rate expectations, the dollar and gold. US inflation data for October is out on 10 November (BLS), and the next Fed decision is on 9 December (Federal Reserve).
