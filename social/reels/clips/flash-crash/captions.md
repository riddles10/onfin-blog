# Clip reel: The 2010 flash crash, live squawk

Source: S&P 500 futures flash crash replay with CME squawk audio, 6 May 2010, Mantiqo YouTube channel, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v6Pa6i-EKmk (3:06 to 3:23, 3:45 to 3:52 and 4:37 to 4:45).

## instagram

6 May 2010, the flash crash. The Dow fell nearly 1,000 points within minutes, then won back most of the drop the same afternoon.

At 2:45:28 pm ET, CME's Stop Logic paused S&P 500 E-mini futures for five seconds. Prices began to recover after the pause (SEC and CFTC joint report, 30 Sep 2010).

Spreads can widen sharply and stops can fill far from where you set them when liquidity disappears.
