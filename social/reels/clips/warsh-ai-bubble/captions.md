At his July 2026 testimony, a senator asked Fed chair Kevin Warsh what would happen to the economy, and to Americans' retirement savings, if an AI bubble popped.

Warsh said he is "not in the business of providing a Wall Street newsletter," but that "booms and busts do not help the real economy." He called the surge in AI companies' investment and valuations "notable."

For AI stocks, the next big test is Nvidia's third-quarter results on 17 November (CNBC).
