# Clip reel: Powell: we do not need to be in a hurry

Source: Senate Banking Committee hearing, Semiannual Monetary Policy Report to Congress, 11 February 2025, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v3LsaWT3HFA (31:39 to 32:05).

## instagram

11 February 2025. After a full percentage point of rate cuts since September 2024, Jerome Powell told the Senate the Fed did "not need to be in a hurry" to adjust rates.

The Fed then left rates unchanged at its next four meetings and cut again in September 2025 (Federal Reserve).

When a central bank signals patience, traders often push back their timing for cuts, and that can move bond yields and the dollar.
