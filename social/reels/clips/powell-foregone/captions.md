# Clip reel: Powell, "not a foregone conclusion, far from it"

Source: Federal Reserve press conference, 29 October 2025, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gZsAKn1UtH4 (5:11 to 5:32).

## instagram

29 October 2025. The Fed had just cut rates, and markets saw another cut in December as close to certain, with nearly 90 percent odds on CME FedWatch.

Then Jerome Powell said a December cut was "not a foregone conclusion, far from it." Within the press conference the odds slid to about 60 percent (CME FedWatch, via Kpler).

The Fed did cut again on 10 December 2025, to 3.50 to 3.75 percent, in a 9 to 3 vote (Federal Reserve, Reuters). Priced in is not the same as decided.
