# Clip reel: Warsh at Jackson Hole 2026: don't call it forward guidance

Source: Jackson Hole Economic Policy Symposium, 28 August 2026, remarks by Fed chair Kevin Warsh, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0UIKsanJb84 (5:06 to 5:16 and 15:30 to 15:52).

## instagram

For years, Fed chairs used "forward guidance" to hint at their next move. At his first Jackson Hole as chair, Kevin Warsh made it clear he wants less of it.

He said guidance in 2021 "might well have slowed the policy response to high inflation."

Less guidance can mean bigger market moves on data days, because less is priced in ahead of time. Three weeks later the Fed raised rates (Federal Reserve, 16 Sep 2026).
