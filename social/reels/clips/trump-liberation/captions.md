# Clip reel: "Liberation Day" tariff chart

Source: The White House, President Trump Participates in the Make America Wealthy Again Event, 2 April 2025, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yiTFPN6SZsU (11:20 to 11:26, 30:23 to 30:36, 30:49 to 30:59 and 35:45 to 35:50).

## instagram

2 April 2025, the White House Rose Garden. President Trump held up a chart of "reciprocal" tariffs, including 34 percent on China and a 10 percent baseline on other countries.

Stocks fell hard. The S&P 500 lost more than 10 percent from 2 to 4 April. On 9 April most of the new tariffs were paused for 90 days, and the index jumped 9.5 percent that day, its biggest one-day gain since 2008 (AP, Russell Investments).

Policy headlines can move markets fast in both directions. Gaps and slippage are real risks in weeks like that.
