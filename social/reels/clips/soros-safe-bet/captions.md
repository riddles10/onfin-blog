# Clip reel: Druckenmiller on sizing the pound trade

Source: Adam Smith's Money World (PBS), via Investor Archive, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LJlXwF7ap7I (3:24 to 4:00).

## instagram

In 1992 George Soros's Quantum Fund bet against the British pound with about one and a half times the fund's entire value, as Stanley Druckenmiller, who ran the trade, describes here.

But he says the risk of loss was only about 2 to 2.5 percent, so the fund risked less than 4 percent. "It wouldn't have killed us."

A big position is not the same as a big risk. What matters is how much you lose if you are wrong, so know that number before you open any trade.
