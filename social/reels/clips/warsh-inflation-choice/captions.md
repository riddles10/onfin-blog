At his confirmation hearing on 21 April 2026, Kevin Warsh set out his view of Fed independence: it is "up to the Fed," and "inflation is the Fed's choice."

He said independence matters most in setting interest rates, and that the Fed should "stay in its lane" on everything else.

As chair he has since dropped forward guidance and raised rates (Federal Reserve, 16 Sep 2026).
