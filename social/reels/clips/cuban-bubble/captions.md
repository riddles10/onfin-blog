# Clip reel: Mark Cuban's bubble test

Source: Lex Fridman Podcast #422 with Mark Cuban (29 March 2024), https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0cn3VBjfN8g (40:06 to 40:20 and 40:32 to 40:52).

## instagram

Mark Cuban's test for a bubble, from the dot-com years: companies with "just a website" going public, with "no intrinsic value at all," trying "to leverage the frothiness of the stock market."

Asked in March 2024 whether AI was in a bubble, he said not yet. A rush of AI companies going public that are just "skins on other people's models" would be "the start of a bubble."

Do you see those signs today?
