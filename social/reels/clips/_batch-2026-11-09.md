29 October 2025. The US government was shut down, and Jerome Powell said some important federal data had been delayed.

## fed-three-questions (30.0 s)

Hook: The three questions the Fed asks before every rate decision:

Source: Fed Functions: Conducting Monetary Policy, Federal Reserve, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xHQJBNO0yQc (2:16 to 2:46).

Before every rate decision, the FOMC weighs three questions, according to the Fed's own explainer: how the US economy is likely to evolve, what the right policy setting is, and how to explain it to the public.

To answer them, policymakers look at the latest economic data, plus reports and surveys from businesses and financial market contacts.

That is why data days can move rate expectations, the dollar and gold. US inflation data for October is out on 10 November (BLS), and the next Fed decision is on 9 December (Federal Reserve).

## soros-safe-bet (35.3 s)

Hook: Stanley Druckenmiller on why betting 1.5 times the fund was a safe bet:

Source: Adam Smith's Money World (PBS), via Investor Archive, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LJlXwF7ap7I (3:24 to 4:00).

In 1992 George Soros's Quantum Fund bet against the British pound with about one and a half times the fund's entire value, as Stanley Druckenmiller, who ran the trade, describes here.

But he says the risk of loss was only about 2 to 2.5 percent, so the fund risked less than 4 percent. "It wouldn't have killed us."

A big position is not the same as a big risk. What matters is how much you lose if you are wrong, so know that number before you open any trade.

## powell-not-allowed (26.0 s)

Hook: Can a president remove a Fed governor? Jerome Powell's answer in 2025:

Source: Senate Banking Committee hearing, Semiannual Monetary Policy Report to Congress, 11 February 2025, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v3LsaWT3HFA (1:06:02 to 1:06:19 and 1:06:25 to 1:06:30 and 1:06:31 to 1:06:36).

11 February 2025. At the Senate, Jerome Powell said "it's not the Fed's job to make or comment on tariff policy." The Fed's job, he said, is to react to it.

Then Senator Jack Reed asked what he would do if the president tried to remove a member of the Fed Board. His answer: "It's pretty clearly not allowed under the law."

Under the Federal Reserve Act, a governor can be removed only "for cause" (Section 10). Markets watch Fed independence closely, because it shapes how investors price inflation, bonds and the dollar.

## ackman-ggp (32.1 s)

Hook: Bill Ackman on the stock that fell from $63 to 34 cents:

In the 2008 financial crisis, the market for commercial mortgage-backed securities shut, and General Growth Properties, the second-largest US mall owner, could not refinance its debt.

The stock fell from $63 a share to 34 cents, Bill Ackman says. His fund started buying in November 2008, because he thought the assets were worth "substantially more than the liabilities." Lex Fridman cites reports that it turned $60 million into over $3 billion.

Ackman calls it an investment, not a trade. Most stocks that fall 99% never come back, and buying one takes deep research into what a company owns and owes.

At his confirmation hearing on 21 April 2026, a senator asked Kevin Warsh whether the Fed has the legal right to issue a central bank digital currency.

Warsh: "I agree that they don't have the right, and I think it would be a bad policy choice." He also agreed not to move towards one, "if it's within the power of the chairman."

A January 2025 executive order already bars federal agencies from establishing or issuing a CBDC (Executive Order 14178). Stablecoins and bitcoin are private assets, not central bank money.
