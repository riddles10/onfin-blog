# Clip reel: Soros and Black Wednesday

Source: Adam Smith's Money World (PBS), via Investor Archive, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LJlXwF7ap7I (1:48 to 1:59 and 2:03 to 2:34).

## instagram

On 16 September 1992, Black Wednesday, the UK pulled the pound out of the European Exchange Rate Mechanism after failing to defend it.

George Soros's Quantum Fund had bet heavily against the pound and was reported to have made about $1 billion.

Few traders get a setup like that, and big bets can go badly wrong just as fast. Know the full size of your downside before you open any position.
