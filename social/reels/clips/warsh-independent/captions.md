At his first semiannual testimony as Fed chair in July 2026, a senator asked Kevin Warsh whether he has spoken with President Trump since taking the job.

He would not share the discussions: "They chose an independent guy to do an independent job, and that's exactly what I plan on doing."

Markets watch every sign of central bank independence, because it shapes how investors price inflation, bonds and the dollar.
