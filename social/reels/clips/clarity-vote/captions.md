# Clip reel: Senate committee advances the CLARITY Act, 15 to 9

Source: Senate Banking Committee markup of H.R. 3633, the Digital Asset Market Clarity Act of 2025, 14 May 2026, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VyO6K2Yqvn8 (2:13:39 to 2:13:49 and 2:14:54 to 2:15:02).

## instagram

14 May 2026: the Senate Banking Committee voted 15 to 9 to send H.R. 3633, the Digital Asset Market Clarity Act, to the full Senate.

The bill sets out how digital assets would be regulated in the US, including which fall under the SEC and which under the CFTC. Two Democrats, Ruben Gallego and Angela Alsobrooks, voted in favor.

Crypto prices often react to regulatory headlines like this one, in both directions.
