Mark Cuban sold Broadcast.com to Yahoo in 1999 for $5.7 billion in stock. Instead of riding the dot-com bubble higher, he hedged his Yahoo shares to protect what he had.

"How much money do I need?"

When the bubble burst, Yahoo's stock fell more than 90% from its peak.

Knowing when you have enough is a risk decision too. Would you have held on?
