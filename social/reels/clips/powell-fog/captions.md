# Clip reel: Powell: driving in the fog, you slow down

Source: Federal Reserve press conference, 29 October 2025, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gZsAKn1UtH4 (27:18 to 27:23 and 27:26 to 27:38 and 27:43 to 27:50 and 27:54 to 27:58).

## instagram

29 October 2025. The US government was shut down, and Jerome Powell said some important federal data had been delayed.

Asked whether that could affect the December decision, he said: "What do you do if you're driving in the fog? You slow down." He added he was not committing to it.

The Fed did cut again on 10 December 2025, to 3.50 to 3.75 percent (Federal Reserve). Missing data tends to make central banks more cautious, and makes markets react harder to the data that does arrive.
