Bill Ackman's biggest loss: Valeant Pharmaceuticals, about $4 billion.

He joined the board to fix it, found the mess was bigger than it looked from the outside, and was stuck. Once the market lost confidence in management, the stock was crushed.

Even the best-known investors take big losses. What decides whether you survive them is how much you had riding on one position.
