Source: Federal Reserve press conference, 28 October 2026, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hs8YWtNQQ40 (18:25 to 19:06).

The Fed held rates at 3.75 to 4 percent on 28 October. At the press conference, a reporter read Kevin Warsh a quote from President Trump saying the Fed board "would like to see the country do badly."

Warsh would not comment on the quote. He said the committee "just came out of a heck of a family fight" before reaching its decision.

Rate decisions move the dollar, gold and stock indices. The next scheduled FOMC meeting is 8 and 9 December.
