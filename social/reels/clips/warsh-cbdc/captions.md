Source: Senate Banking Committee hearing on the nomination of Kevin Warsh, 21 April 2026, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lp8iT04clIM (2:44:34 to 2:44:48 and 2:44:50 to 2:45:03).

At his confirmation hearing on 21 April 2026, a senator asked Kevin Warsh whether the Fed has the legal right to issue a central bank digital currency.

Warsh: "I agree that they don't have the right, and I think it would be a bad policy choice." He also agreed not to move towards one, "if it's within the power of the chairman."

A January 2025 executive order already bars federal agencies from establishing or issuing a CBDC (Executive Order 14178). Stablecoins and bitcoin are private assets, not central bank money.
