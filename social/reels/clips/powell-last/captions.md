29 April 2026: Jerome Powell's last press conference as Fed chair. He congratulated Kevin Warsh on clearing the Senate Banking Committee that morning.

His term as chair ended on 15 May. He said he would stay on as a governor "for a period of time to be determined," with "only ever one chair."

A change at the top of the Fed is one of the biggest scheduled events for rates, the dollar and gold.
