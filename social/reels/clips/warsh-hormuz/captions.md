On 16 September 2026 the Fed raised its target range by a quarter point to 3.75 to 4 percent (Federal Reserve).

A reporter pointed out that a rate hike cannot fix an oil supply shock. Kevin Warsh agreed the Fed cannot set any single price, oil or groceries. The goal, he said, is to stop those price jumps from spreading through the rest of the economy.

That is why oil shocks and rate decisions often move markets together.
