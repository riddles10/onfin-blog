At his first press conference as Fed chair on 17 June 2026, Kevin Warsh dropped forward guidance from the statement. But the dot plot showed nine officials expecting a rate increase by year end.

His take on those projections: they were submitted with "pencils, those kind with the big erasers."

The dot plot is a set of forecasts, not a promise. Rates went up three months later (Federal Reserve, 16 Sep 2026).
