# Clip reel: Soros on getting the 1987 crash wrong

Source: Adam Smith's Money World (PBS), via Investor Archive, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LJlXwF7ap7I (11:05 to 11:39).

## instagram

George Soros saw a crash coming in 1987. He expected it to start in Japan. It started on Wall Street instead, on Black Monday, 19 October 1987.

"So I was wrong." Some estimated the mistake cost him $650 million.

Even the best-known traders get calls wrong. What matters is having a plan for when you do.
