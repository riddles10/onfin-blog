# OnFin Academy: first-week organic plan

**Day 1 is the day academy.onfin.io goes live.** Nothing is scheduled yet. Don't post until the site works, because every post links to it.

**Times are Cambodia time (ICT, UTC+7), with UTC in brackets.**

**Posting caps** (from `social/_schedule/README.md`): each Telegram channel 2 a day; Facebook 2 a day plus the giveaway; Instagram 4 a day, with at most 2 feed posts at least 3 hours apart. The caps apply to each account, including the new Academy ones. On any day where an existing OnFin channel would go over its cap, move that day's evergreen post to the next free day.

**The @onfinkm feed block is still open.** On @onfinkm we use only Collab reels (posted from @onfinacademy) and stories. Carousels and statics go out from @onfinacademy, which is a new account and isn't blocked.

**Formats per platform:**
- **Instagram:** reels, the carousel and statics as they are.
- **Facebook:** reels as native video. The carousel goes out as a slideshow video (our Facebook format), built with `social/_templates/slideshow/slideshow.py` from `carousel/en` or `carousel/km`.
- **Telegram:** text first, then one image.

The reels and trailer come from the video fork, in `reels/`.
