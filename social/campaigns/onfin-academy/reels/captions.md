**EN**

## 2. The spread

**EN**

Why does every trade start slightly negative? You buy at the ask, but your position is valued at the bid. The price has to move at least the width of the spread in your favour before you break even.

## 3. Leverage

**EN**

With $1,000 at 1:100, you control about $100,000. A 1% move against you is about $1,000: the whole account. Leverage changes what you put down, not what you can lose.

From lesson 7 of Forex basics, a free course of 12 short lessons. Start at academy.onfin.io

Example figures, for illustration. Check your own account's leverage terms.

High risk: most retail CFD accounts lose money. Education, not investment advice.

#forex #tradingeducation #OnFinAcademy

## 4. Margin call and stop-out

**EN**

A margin call is a warning: your free margin is zero and you can't open new trades. A stop-out is when the broker starts closing your positions for you. The levels here, 100% and 50%, are common examples, so check your own account's levels.

From lesson 9 of Forex basics, a free course of 12 short lessons. Start at academy.onfin.io

High risk: most retail CFD accounts lose money. Education, not investment advice.

## 5. Trading sessions

**EN**

The busiest hours in forex are when London and New York overlap, from about 13:00 to 17:00 UTC. In Cambodia, that's about 8pm to midnight. Spreads on the majors are usually tightest then, and they often widen in the quiet hours after New York closes.

From lesson 10 of Forex basics, a free course of 12 short lessons. Start at academy.onfin.io

Session times are approximate and shift by an hour when daylight saving starts or ends.

High risk: most retail CFD accounts lose money. Education, not investment advice.

## 6. Stop loss and take profit

**EN**

A stop loss closes a trade at a loss level you chose. A take profit closes it at your target. In fast markets, or over a weekend gap, a stop can fill at a worse price than you set, so it's a limit on your plan, not a guarantee. Know your exit before you enter.

From lesson 11 of Forex basics, a free course of 12 short lessons. Start at academy.onfin.io

High risk: most retail CFD accounts lose money. Education, not investment advice.

#forex #tradingeducation #OnFinAcademy
