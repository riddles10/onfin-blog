## facebook

Every forex trade starts with one number that decides what each pip is worth: the lot size.

A standard lot is 100,000 units of the base currency, so one lot of EUR/USD means buying €100,000. On EUR/USD a pip is worth $10 on a standard lot, $1 on a mini, $0.10 on a micro and $0.01 on a nano.

That gap adds up fast on a small account. A 20-pip stop with a standard lot costs $200, which is 40% of a $500 account. The same stop on a micro lot costs $2 (OnFin calculation). Leverage only changes how much margin the trade ties up; it doesn't make a big lot safe to hold.

Sizing a trade takes four steps: set your risk ($20 on a $1,000 account at 2%), measure the stop (say 30 pips), divide ($20 ÷ (30 × $1 per mini lot) = 0.66 mini lots) and round down (6 micro lots, $18 at risk).
