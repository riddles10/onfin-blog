## facebook

Margin is a deposit, not free money, and it magnifies losses just as much as gains.

To trade $100,000 of currency with a 1% margin requirement, you put up $1,000. The other $99,000 is effectively borrowed from the broker.

Here is how that cuts both ways on a $50,000 EUR/USD position at a 2% margin requirement, a $1,000 deposit. A 1% move in your favour makes $500, or 50% of the margin. A 1% move against you loses $500, and a 2% move against you loses the whole $1,000 (OnFin example, before spreads and costs).

As losses come out of your account, a margin call asks you to add funds. If you don't, the position can be closed automatically.

Three ways to start carefully: skip the maximum leverage until you see how positions react, practise on a demo account first, and learn what triggers a margin call before you go live.

## telegram-en

**Margin in forex, explained**

Margin is a deposit, not free money. It magnifies losses just as much as gains.

• At a 1% margin requirement, $1,000 opens a $100,000 position; the rest is effectively borrowed from the broker

• $50,000 EUR/USD at 2% margin ($1,000 deposit): a 1% move is $500, or 50% of the margin. A 2% move against you wipes out the deposit

• Margin call: losses come out of your account, you're asked to add funds, and the position can be closed automatically

• Start carefully: low leverage, a demo account first, a stop-loss on every trade

## telegram-ms

**Margin dalam forex, diterangkan**

Margin ialah deposit, bukan wang percuma. Ia membesarkan kerugian sama seperti keuntungan.

• Dengan keperluan margin 1%, $1,000 membuka posisi $100,000; bakinya pada dasarnya dipinjam daripada broker

• Panggilan margin: kerugian ditolak daripada akaun, anda diminta menambah dana, dan posisi boleh ditutup secara automatik

## telegram-ru

**Маржа на Форексе: объяснение**

Маржа это залог, а не бесплатные деньги. Она увеличивает убытки так же, как и прибыль.

• При требовании маржи 1% $1 000 открывают позицию на $100 000; остальное фактически занято у брокера

• $50 000 в EUR/USD при марже 2% (залог $1 000): движение на 1% это $500, или 50% маржи. Движение на 2% против вас обнуляет залог

• Маржин-колл: убытки списываются со счёта, вас просят внести средства, а позицию могут закрыть автоматически

• Начинайте осторожно: низкое плечо, сначала демосчёт, стоп-лосс в каждой сделке

## telegram-es

**El margen en Forex, explicado**

El margen es un depósito, no dinero gratis. Amplifica las pérdidas tanto como las ganancias.

• Con un requisito de margen del 1%, $1,000 abren una posición de $100,000; el resto en la práctica lo presta el bróker

• $50,000 en EUR/USD con margen del 2% (depósito de $1,000): un movimiento del 1% son $500, el 50% del margen. Un 2% en contra borra el depósito

• Llamada de margen: las pérdidas salen de tu cuenta, te piden añadir fondos y la posición puede cerrarse automáticamente

• Empieza con cuidado: poco apalancamiento, primero una cuenta demo y un stop-loss en cada operación
