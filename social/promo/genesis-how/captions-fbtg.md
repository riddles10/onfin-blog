## facebook

Genesis pays creators for the views their OnFin videos get. It's free to join, and you don't need to trade or deposit.

It works in five steps. Apply once and link your accounts and a USDT (TRC-20) wallet. Prove your account with a short code in your bio. Get your script, then your draft, approved. Post the video with the tag, hashtags and #ad line and send us the link. Then get paid weekly in USDT once the views are counted.

Videos earn $3 per 1,000 views, from 5,000 views and up to $100 each, and each brief sets its own rate, minimum and cap. On one live brief, 19,300 views earned $57.90.

Three things cost you the payout: promising profits or giving investment advice, buying views or reposting someone else's video, and deleting a video before its views lock.
